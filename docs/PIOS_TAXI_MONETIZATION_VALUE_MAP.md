# PIOS Taxi — Monetization Value Map

**Date:** 2026-09-15. **Status:** Analysis only — no billing, no subscription, no paywall is implemented or authorized by this document. Per `docs/PIOS_TAXI_PRODUCT_COMPLETION_AUDIT.md` Section 11: zero monetization infrastructure exists anywhere in the codebase today, confirmed by full repository search. This document answers a narrower question — **which already-real features create value a driver would plausibly pay to keep** — without inventing a subscription, price, or tier. Model: `Driver → PIOS subscription → access to business infrastructure`. PIOS takes no per-ride commission; the driver keeps the ride's price in full, always.

---

| FEATURE | DRIVER VALUE | PASSENGER VALUE | BUSINESS RESULT | WHY DRIVER WOULD PAY |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Personal invite link / QR** (`QRCard`, driver's own code) | A direct channel to their own clients, bypassing any pool | Reaches a specific, known driver directly | Every order through this link is attributable to the driver's own effort, not PIOS's matching | This is the infrastructure that makes "my own business, not a gig" real — without it, a driver has no distribution channel of their own at all |
| **Circle of Trust / Primary Driver** (ADR-054, ADR-062) | A passenger who names them primary keeps coming back to them specifically, not a pool | Never has to re-explain "who I usually ride with"; one designated go-to driver | Retention, not one-off transactions | A driver pays to keep the mechanism that turns a first ride into a standing relationship, not a memory |
| **Trusted-first Fallback Dispatch** (ADR-068, live and proven this session) | When they're unavailable, their own passenger still lands with someone the passenger already trusts, not a stranger — the driver's own network absorbs the miss, not PIOS's general pool | Never falls out of their own trusted circle just because one driver was busy | Keeps a driver's client relationships intact even through unavailability | This is the clearest "PIOS protects my client base" feature in the product — worth paying to keep active |
| **Channel 1 discovery matching** (this Sprint, ADR-070) | The only mechanism today that can bring a client the driver did **not** already know | A passenger with no driver yet still gets one, without browsing a catalog | Genuine new-client acquisition, not just retention of existing relationships | The single strongest future subscription pitch: "PIOS is where my next new client comes from," not just "where my existing ones stay" |
| **"Мой бизнес" dashboard** (completed rides, streak, repeat clients, new clients, lifetime referred, stated earnings) | Sees, in one honest screen, whether their business is actually growing — no fabricated numbers, no hidden zeros | — | Turns abstract activity into a visible growth narrative the driver can act on | Analytics over their own business is exactly the kind of thing an independent contractor already expects to pay a tool for (the same category as any small-business dashboard) |
| **"Мои пассажиры" client list** | A named, revisitable list of who their clients actually are, not just a ride log | — | The literal client base the product promise is built around | This is the product's own definition of "your own client base" made visible — the clearest candidate for a "this is what you're renting access to" pitch |
| **Ride History** (`RideHistoryCard`) | A real record of completed work — date, route, client, price | A record they can refer back to | Trust and bookkeeping, not just memory | Lower-tier value on its own, but combined with the dashboard it's the record a driver would want to keep even between PIOS sessions |

## What this map deliberately does not do

It does not propose a price, a free/paid feature split, or a tier boundary — inventing any of those would be exactly the fabricated business rule `CLAUDE.md` forbids. The next real step, per the audit's own Section 11 recommendation, is a ratified Product Decision naming what specifically moves behind a paywall (if anything) versus what stays free as the baseline "a driver can always earn through PIOS" guarantee the product promise requires — not assumed or inferred here.
